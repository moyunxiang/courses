Common Mistakes

  • Temperatures in K for pV=nRT, ΔS=Q/T, Carnot and COP (1−2037 is wrong).
  • Sign of work: course uses Won in ΔU=Q+Won; expansion ⇒ Won<0.
  • Adiabatic exponents: TVγ−1, pVγ; Otto uses rγ−1.
  • Otto burns at constant volume, Diesel and Brayton at constant pressure; cetane = easy ignition (Diesel), octane = knock resistance (petrol).
  • Drag: energy per km ∝ v2, power ∝ v3; use CdA, not A; km/h → m/s (÷ 3.6).
  • J/m → kWh/km: × 1000 ÷ 3.6×10⁶; N/kg → kWh/(t·km): ÷ 3.6.
  • Transmission loss uses the line current I=P/V (not V2/R with the full line voltage).
  • Wind / water power ∝ v3: average v3, not cube the average speed.
  • Load factor and efficiency are different multipliers — apply both when asked for annual energy.
  • Mass defect: parents minus products, include every neutron; MeV ↔ J with 1.6×10⁻¹³.
