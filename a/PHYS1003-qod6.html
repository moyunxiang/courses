Given. Q=100 J flows from cold reservoir TC=350 K to hot reservoir TH=650 K.

Reservoirs at constant T: ΔS=Q/T; rod in steady state: ΔSrod=0.

(a) Reversible, irreversible or impossible?

ΔStotal=ΔSH+ΔSC=QHTH+QCTC=+100 J650 K+−100 J350 K=0.1538 J/K−0.2857 J/K=−0.1319 J/K<0

2nd Law: ΔStotal≥0 for any process (=0 reversible, >0 irreversible).

ΔStotal≈−0.132 J/K<0 ⇒ impossible

(b) TC for a reversible transfer

Reversible ⇔ΔStotal=0, with TH=650 K, Q≠0:

ΔStotal=QTH−QTC=0⇒1TC=1TH⇒TC=TH
TC=650 K
  • Reversible heat transfer only in the limit of zero temperature difference.
