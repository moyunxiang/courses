Q4

Use Tr⁡(ST)=Tr⁡(TS) for S,T∈ℒ(V,V) (matrices in a fixed basis).

(a)

K(A,B)=Tr⁡(ad⁡A∘ad⁡B)=Tr⁡(ad⁡B∘ad⁡A)=K(B,A)

∎

(b)

ad⁡A is linear: ad⁡A(cB+B′)=A(cB+B′)−(cB+B′)A=cad⁡AB+ad⁡AB′.

Linear in A:

ad⁡cA+A′(B)=(cA+A′)B−B(cA+A′)=cad⁡A(B)+ad⁡A′(B) ⇒ ad⁡cA+A′=cad⁡A+ad⁡A′
K(cA+A′,B)=Tr⁡((cad⁡A+ad⁡A′)∘ad⁡B)=cK(A,B)+K(A′,B)

Linear in B by (a). ∎

(c)

For all C:

(ad⁡Aad⁡B−ad⁡Bad⁡A)(C)=A(BC−CB)−(BC−CB)A−B(AC−CA)+(AC−CA)B=ABC−BAC−CAB+CBA=[A,B]C−C[A,B]=ad⁡[A,B](C)
K([A,B],C)=Tr⁡(ad⁡Aad⁡Bad⁡C)−Tr⁡(ad⁡Bad⁡Aad⁡C)K(A,[B,C])=Tr⁡(ad⁡Aad⁡Bad⁡C)−Tr⁡(ad⁡Aad⁡Cad⁡B)

Tr⁡(ad⁡B⋅ad⁡Aad⁡C)=Tr⁡(ad⁡Aad⁡C⋅ad⁡B)⇒K([A,B],C)=K(A,[B,C]). ∎
