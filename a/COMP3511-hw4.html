2) Two-level paging and the TLB

(a) d=log⁡28192=13; one inner table = 8 KB/4 B=211 entries → p2=11; p1=32−11−13=8. p1 = 8 bits, p2 = 11 bits, d = 13 bits

(b) One inner table maps 211×8 KB=16 MB. Code: 64/16=4 inner tables; stack: 8 MB lies in the top 16 MB → 1 inner table; plus 1 outer table. Total =6×8 KB= 48 KB. Single-level: 232−13×4 B=219×4 B= 2 MB.

(c) Hit: 5+100=105 ns; miss: 5+3×100=305 ns. (i) EAT=0.95×105+0.05×305=99.75+15.25= 115 ns (ii) 105h+305(1−h)≤110⇒305−200h≤110⇒h≥195/200= 0.975 (97.5%)
