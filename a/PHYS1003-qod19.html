10 kWh/L12 km/L=𝟎.𝟖𝟑 𝐤𝐖𝐡/𝐤𝐦 (per car)

Alone in the car, walking is more efficient (about 3 times less source energy).

Number of people: per-person car energy is 0.83/n kWh/km, so the car wins when

0.83n<0.27  ⇒  n>3.05  ⇒  𝐧=𝟒 people (including the driver)

(Extra passengers add little, since the car's own mass dominates the losses.)
