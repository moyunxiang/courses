Common Mistakes

  1. f(at+b): shift is ba, not b — shift first, then scale.
  2. δ(at)=1∣a∣δ(t) (absolute value); ∫fδ′=−f′(0) (minus sign).
  3. DT period must be an integer: sin⁡0.4n aperiodic, sin⁡0.4t periodic.
  4. Periodic power: one period ÷T; P{Acos⁡}=A22, not A2.
  5. y=2x+1: not linear (x=0⇒y=1), but TI.
  6. x(2t), x(−t): time-varying and non-causal (y(1)=x(2), y(−1)=x(1)).
  7. Piecewise convolution: write ends t−D, t−C of h(t−τ) first; don't drop end intervals.
  8. Accumulator / integrator: LTI, causal, unstable.
  9. Phase in (−π,π] (or as specified); ∣f∣≥0 always.
  10. ⚠️ Ch3: u(t)↔πδ(ω)+1jω (don't drop πδ); ∫f↔Fjω+πF(0)δ(ω); FS ck=2∣Fk∣ for k≥1.
