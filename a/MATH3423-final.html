Common Mistakes

  1. t(n−1) only for a normal sample; non-normal large n uses z via Slutsky (needs μ4<∞ for S→pσ).
  2. Degrees of freedom: ∑(Xi−Xˉ)2/σ2∼χ2(n−1), but ∑(Xi−μ)2/σ2∼χ2(n).
  3. Slutsky needs Wn→p a constant; state CLT, WLLN/CMT, Slutsky each by name.
  4. Delta method variance is [g′(a)]2b2; if g′(a)=0 switch to 2nd order (scale n, limit χ2(1)).
  5. Decreasing back-transform (e−eg, log-log CI) swaps the endpoints.
  6. MLE: a root of ℓ′=0 is only a candidate — check it lies in Θ, check boundaries, justify the global max; support depending on θ (uniform) ⇒ no derivative, no Fisher-information asymptotics.
  7. I𝐗=nIX1; asymptotic variance of θ^ is 1nIX1; minus sign in −E[ℓ′′].
  8. LRT: θ^ is maximized over Θ0∪Θ1 (e.g. [θ0,∞)), not the whole real line; size is set at the boundary of Θ0.
  9. Say "do not reject H0", never "accept H0"; "=" belongs to H0; zα is the upper-tail point.
  10. KM: nj = at risk just before t(j) (remove earlier deaths and censored); censored times give no step; S^=0 after t(r) only if no censored time ≥t(r).
