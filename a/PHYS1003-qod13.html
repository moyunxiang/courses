Given. A=200 m2, v=3 m/s, h=10 m, efficiency =100%; ρ=1000 kg/m3, g≈10 m/s2 (Lecture 13). Flow =Av=200×3=600 m3/s.

(a) Run-of-the-river (kinetic energy of the flow):

P=12ρAv3=12(1000)(200)(3)3=2.7×106 W=𝟐.𝟕 𝐌𝐖

(b) Impoundment (potential energy over the head):

P=ρgh×flow=(1000)(10)(10)(600)=6.0×107 W=𝟔𝟎 𝐌𝐖

Choose the impoundment plant: it gives about 60/2.7≈22 times more power, because the 10 m head (gh=100 J/kg) stores far more energy per kilogram of water than the flow speed (12v2=4.5 J/kg).
