(g) What the bandpass does

Passband (5–9 kHz): the 7 kHz component passes with ∣H∣≈1 (amplitude 0.4 kept); only its phase shifts by −0.219π (a delay). Stopband: 300 Hz and 12 kHz get ∣H∣≈0, so they are removed; after the start-up transient y is a single 7 kHz cosine.
