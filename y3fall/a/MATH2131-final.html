Common Mistakes

  • Eigenvector must be nonzero; λ=0 is allowed.
  • A diagonalizable ⇏ distinct eigenvalues (e.g. I); distinct ⇒ diagonalizable only one way.
  • Over ℝ, rotation-type matrices have no eigenvalues; say which field (HW04: "diagonalization only over ℝ").
  • Orthogonal diagonalization: Gram–Schmidt within each eigenspace only; mixing eigenspaces destroys eigenvectors.
  • SVD: ui=Avi/σi — do not compute U and V independently (sign mismatch). Σ is m×n.
  • Complex inner product: conjugate in the 2nd slot; ⟨v,cw⟩=cˉ⟨v,w⟩; (cT)∗=cˉT∗.
  • Hermitian ⇒ unitarily diagonalizable, but the converse needs real eigenvalues; normal is the exact condition (Thm 7.22).
  • Minimal polynomial must contain every eigenvalue (Prop 8.12); deg⁡m≤n.
  • Jordan: # blocks = geometric multiplicity, not algebraic; largest block = exponent in m.
  • RCF invariant factors divide each other in increasing order; m=gk is the last one.
  • Quotient maps: always check well-definedness before linearity.
  • Dual map T∗:W∗→V∗ goes the opposite direction; matrix = transpose (no conjugate).
