Common Mistakes

  • Columns (not rows) of [T]ℬ′ℬ are [Tbj]ℬ′; match P=Pℬ′ℬ with [T]ℬ′=P[T]ℬP−1.
  • Basis of Col⁡A = pivot columns of the original A, not of RREF.
  • ≥3 subspaces: pairwise Ui∩Uj={0} is not enough for ⨁.
  • dim⁡Kn[t]=n+1.
  • ri↦cri multiplies det by c; det⁡(cA)=cndet⁡A; swap ×(−1).
  • Cramer needs det⁡A≠0.
  • ⚠️ 12 (T4) and nλ=0 need 2≠0, n≠0 in K (true for ℚ,ℝ,ℂ; careful if "any field").
  • A(ATA)−1AT needs independent columns; normalize before PPT.
  • Reproving det⁡A=det⁡AT: 1=det⁡(QTQ)=(det⁡Q)2 is circular (HW03 Q3(d)).
  • Ch 1–2 proofs: no determinants / eigenvalues; HW01 official solution: "do not use determinant to show invertibility".
