Assumptions (from Lecture 18). Shower flow 10 L/min, water heated from 15∘C to 40∘C; coffee maker 500 W; minibus 0.12 kWh/person-km; noodle water boiled from 20∘C to 100∘C; cwater=4.2 kJ/(kg K); 1 kWh=3600 kJ. The laptop is also used during the 5 evening hours.

Showers (2 × 10 min, m=100 kg each):

Q=2×100×4.2×(40−15)=21000 kJ=5.83 kWh

Coffee maker: 0.5 kW×16 h=0.083 kWh

Minibus (2 × 10 km): 20×0.12=2.40 kWh

Laptop (5 h day + 5 h evening): 0.03 kW×10 h=0.30 kWh

LED lamp: 0.01 kW×5 h=0.05 kWh

Cup noodles (2 × 0.6 L):

Q=2×0.6×4.2×(100−20)=403 kJ=0.11 kWh

Total:

E=5.83+0.083+2.40+0.30+0.05+0.11≈𝟖.𝟖 𝐤𝐖𝐡 (≈32 MJ)

The two hot showers dominate (about two thirds of the total).
