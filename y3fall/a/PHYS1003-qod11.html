Given. B points down, ΔB/Δt=0.05 T/s (decreasing); diameter 20 cm so r=0.10 m; N=25000; R=2 Ω.

(a) Clockwise (top view).

Lenz's law: the downward flux is decreasing, so the induced current must create its own downward field inside the pot; by the right-hand rule a current seen clockwise from above produces a field pointing down.
