(b) Yes, it is partly too good to be true:

  1. Neutron damage and activation. The 14 MeV neutrons embrittle the reactor wall and make it radioactive, so wall parts must be replaced often and become radioactive waste.
  2. Tritium supply and leakage. Tritium must be bred from lithium inside the reactor at a breeding ratio above 1, which is unproven, and tritium is radioactive and leaks through metals easily.
