(a)

  1. Acid dissolves limestone and marble, damaging buildings and statues: CaCO3+H2SO4→CaSO4+H2O+CO2.
  2. Acid corrodes metal structures such as bridges, railings and cars.

(b)

  1. Take the MTR instead of a car or taxi. Vehicle exhaust is a main local source of NOx and PM, and the MTR runs on electricity, so it emits nothing on the street.
  2. Save electricity. Less coal and gas is burned in power plants, so less SO2, NOx and PM is emitted.
